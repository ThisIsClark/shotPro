FormRefine Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026.

What happens to your shooting video

When you upload a clip, FormRefine processes it to identify shooting phases, estimate joint angles and generate your report. Reports can contain recognizable still images, a short silent replay of the analyzed shot, and measurements. The replay follows the same access and deletion rules as the report. The analyzer removes the source upload after processing. Saved reports and their images remain available in your analysis history until deleted. Guest reports expire unless saved to an account.

Your account and Google sign-in

We use account identifiers and email addresses to sign you in and associate reports with your account. If you choose Google sign-in where available, we use the identifier and profile information Google shares, including your email, name and profile picture, to identify your account and display your profile. To request deletion of this information and your account, contact us below.

Site operation and subscriptions

Cookies and browser storage help maintain sessions, enforce free-trial limits and control access to reports. We process technical and usage information such as IP addresses, browser details and activity timestamps to operate, secure and improve the service. If you subscribe, Creem handles checkout. We use transaction references and subscription status to manage access. We do not store your full payment card number.

Service providers

We use Supabase for account and data services on the existing analyzer. Hosting and storage providers process the information needed to deliver the website and reports. Google participates in sign-in when you select that option. Creem processes payments when you use checkout.

Your choices and contact

You can review and delete saved reports in Analysis History. For a copy of your personal information, a correction, account deletion or a privacy question, email ThisIsClark@163.com from the address associated with your account so we can verify your request.
